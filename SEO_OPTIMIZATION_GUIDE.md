# SEO Optimization Guide for Burger Builder

This guide outlines the comprehensive SEO strategy implemented for ranking your Burger Builder app on Google.

## ✅ What We've Implemented

### 1. **Technical SEO**

- ✅ **Structured Data (JSON-LD)**: Added Schema.org markup for Organization, WebSite, and Restaurant
- ✅ **Meta Tags**: Comprehensive meta tags including Open Graph and Twitter Cards
- ✅ **Sitemap**: Auto-generated XML sitemap at `/sitemap.xml`
- ✅ **Robots.txt**: Proper robots.txt configuration
- ✅ **Canonical URLs**: Prevent duplicate content issues
- ✅ **Mobile Optimization**: PWA manifest and responsive design
- ✅ **Page Speed**: Vercel analytics and speed insights enabled

### 2. **On-Page SEO**

- ✅ **Title Tags**: Optimized with keywords
- ✅ **Meta Descriptions**: Compelling descriptions for each page
- ✅ **Keywords**: Targeted food/burger-related keywords
- ✅ **Heading Structure**: Proper H1-H6 hierarchy
- ✅ **Alt Text**: Images have descriptive alt attributes
- ✅ **Internal Linking**: Connected pages structure

### 3. **Content Optimization**

- ✅ **Rich Keywords**: burger builder, custom burger, online ordering, etc.
- ✅ **Localization**: English and French language support
- ✅ **Fresh Content**: Order history, loyalty program, etc.
- ✅ **User Engagement**: Interactive burger builder

## 📊 Key Metrics to Track

### Search Console Metrics:

- **Impressions**: How many times your site appears in search
- **Clicks**: How many people click from search results
- **CTR**: Click-through rate (clicks/impressions)
- **Position**: Average ranking position
- **Core Web Vitals**: LCP, FID, CLS scores

### Analytics Metrics:

- **Organic Traffic**: Visitors from search engines
- **Bounce Rate**: % of single-page visits
- **Session Duration**: Time users spend on site
- **Conversion Rate**: Orders/visits
- **Top Landing Pages**: Most visited pages

4. **Local Matters**: If you have a physical location, optimize for local

7. **Track Everything**: You can't improve what you don't measure
